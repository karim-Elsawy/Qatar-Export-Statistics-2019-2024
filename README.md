# Qatar-Export-Statistics-2019-2024
An interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Qatar's export dynamics, historical trends, commodity breakdown, and trade distribution across key partner nations.


📌 Executive Summary & Key Insights
This analysis explores the structural changes in Qatar's exports over a 6-year period (2019–2024), uncovering major economic shifts driven by global market conditions, pandemic disruptions, and energy market fluctuations:

High Market Concentration: The top 10 trade partners account for ~94% of Qatar's total export value, indicating strong reliance on specific strategic markets.

Peak Export Performance (2022): Exports hit a record peak of QAR 476.7 Billion, registering an exceptional +50.2% YoY growth driven by global energy demand surges.

Quantity vs. Market Value Contrast:

China is the leading market by Value (> QAR 325B with 400M units).

Japan ranks highest by Export Volume/Quantity with over 432 Million units.

Energy Sector Dominance: Petroleum gases and oils dominate export revenue, accounting for > QAR 1.2 Trillion of total export value.

Resilience & Post-Peak Adjustment: After a pandemic-induced contraction in 2020 (-29.4%) and rapid rebound in 2021 (+69.3%), exports normalized in 2023 (-25.4%) and stabilized in 2024 (-2.8% at QAR 345.1B).


## 🛠️ Technical Implementation & Features
Data Modeling & Time Intelligence: Developed robust DAX measures to calculate dynamic Year-over-Year (YoY) growth in terms of absolute monetary value and percentages while handling missing dates/blanks seamlessly.
